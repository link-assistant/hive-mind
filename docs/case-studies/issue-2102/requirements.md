## Known gaps, stated rather than implied

1. **A complete turn that produces no work is still reported as success in the
   general case.** The new gate closes it only for the plugin-rejection cause.
   The incident's most alarming artefact — two `## ✅ Ready to merge — All CI
checks have passed` comments on a PR with `changed_files: 0` — has a broader
   cause than #2102 and deserves its own issue rather than a widened gate
   smuggled in here. Called out in `analysis.md`.
2. **C9's rendered-prompt count** is unmeasurable without Codex auth in this
   environment (`raw/e2e-04-no-auth-limit.log`). The runtime check that would
   catch a regression there is #2084's, and it is now reachable on this path.
3. **Requirements stated in prose we do not parse** (a wiki, a PR template, a
   `CONTRIBUTING.md`) remain undetected by design. `--require-codex-plugin` is
   the escape hatch for that case, which is why C8 is not optional in practice.
