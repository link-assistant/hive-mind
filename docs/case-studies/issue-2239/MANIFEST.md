screenshots that prompted the issue are in a third-party repository that may be
renamed, merged or deleted, so the facts that depend on it are captured here
rather than left as links.

## Reproducing

`url-verification.txt` and `pull-request-facts.json` are regenerated by the
commands above with an authenticated `gh`; both are read-only. They will change
if `Godmy/frontend#2` is merged and the branch deleted, or if the fork is
removed — at which point the copies here become the only record.

## Not kept

The uncompressed log is not committed: it is 3.2 MB of mostly tool chatter,
`gunzip -c` recovers it exactly, and the six lines that matter are quoted in
`solve-log-excerpts.md`.

The three screenshot PNGs from `Godmy/frontend` are not copied here. They are
somebody else's work product, they are not evidence of anything — the images
themselves were always fine, only the URLs naming them were wrong — and
`url-verification.txt` already records their blob SHAs.
